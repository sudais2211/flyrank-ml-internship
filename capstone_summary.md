# Capstone Summary

**Lane:** Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring

**Research question:** Can a leakage-aware machine-learning model improve prioritization of content items for search-performance review compared with a transparent baseline?

**Primary metric:** Precision@50

**Reference result:** Baseline 0.240 vs random forest ~0.740, as documented by the public FlyRank starter guide.

**Honest framing:** This is decision support, not proof of Google's algorithm or causal evidence that refreshing content improves rankings.
